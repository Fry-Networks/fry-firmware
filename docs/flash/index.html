No Android? After flashing choose Connect to Wi-Fi, then Visit Device to register your miner key.

Updating a board that already has a key? Leave Erase unchecked — your key stays, and an older IOT- key converts to FEM- on first boot.

Next: get it earning

Flashing is only the first step — the board still needs your Wi-Fi and a claim on the dashboard. No licence or activation key is required.

  1. Give it Wi-Fi. Either straight from this page with Connect to Wi-Fi, or with the Android app, which also takes your Algorand wallet address.
  2. Note the miner key. It looks like FEM- followed by 32 characters. Visit Device opens the dashboard with it already filled in.
  3. Claim it on the dashboard. Sign in at dashboard.frynetworks.com, connect your wallet, and register that miner key. A device will not appear until you do this — the dashboard lists devices claimed by your account.

Board already set up and not showing in the app? It will not advertise while it is on a Wi-Fi network. Hold BOOT for 10 seconds to factory reset it — it keeps its miner key — then scan again.
